# Changelog

## guluswm (development version)

- First build of the package from the authors’ field data workbook: two
  data objects, `guluswm_survey` (424 households, 43 variables) and
  `guluswm_waste` (95 households, 55 variables), a dictionary with
  drafted descriptions, and `analysis/validate_data.R`, which recomputes
  the figures of the draft manuscript from the data. Household
  coordinates, identifiers carrying enumerator names and the free text
  answers are not included pending the authors’ decision. Draft, not
  released.
